MediTrack 360 🩺🤖

AI-Powered Healthcare Tracking and Management Platform

MediTrack 360 is an AI-powered healthcare platform developed using Google AI Studio and Google Gemini. It aims to simplify health monitoring, organize medical records, and provide AI-assisted health insights through a user-friendly interface.

🚀 Features

Patient Record Management: Add, view, and update patient medical records.

AI-Powered Health Insights: Generate summaries of health information and identify potential trends using Google Gemini.

Health Monitoring: Track vital statistics and display alerts where supported by the application.

User-Friendly Dashboard: Navigate healthcare information through a simple and intuitive interface.

Scalable Design: Allow for future enhancements and additional healthcare features.

🎯 Problem Statement

Managing health information and medical records can be challenging when data is scattered across different systems or maintained manually. This can make it difficult to track health trends and access organized information efficiently.

💡 Our Solution

MediTrack 360 aims to bring healthcare information and AI-assisted insights together in one platform. It is designed to help users organize health records, monitor relevant information, and understand summaries generated by AI.
AI-generated information is intended to support understanding and should not replace professional medical advice or diagnosis.

🛠️ Technology Stack

Development Platform: Google AI Studio

AI Model: Google Gemini

Frontend: Based on the framework and libraries used in the project source code

Version Control: Git and GitHub
